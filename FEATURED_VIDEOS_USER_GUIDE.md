## 📱 How to Access Featured Videos

### For All Users (Free/Silver/Gold):
1. **Open the WGO4Y app**
2. **Go to Home tab**
3. **Scroll to the top** - you'll see "Featured Videos" section
4. **Tap any video** to watch in YouTube/Vimeo

### For Business Owners & Entrepreneurs:
1. **Log in to your account**
2. **Go to Profile → Edit Profile**
3. **Scroll to "Portfolio Videos" section**
4. **Add your video URL (YouTube or Vimeo)**
5. **Click "Feature This Video"** (Silver/Gold only)

## 🎬 VIDEO REQUIREMENTS

### Supported Platforms:
- ✅ YouTube (all standard URLs)
- ✅ Vimeo (all standard URLs)
- ❌ TikTok, Instagram, Facebook (not yet supported)

### Video Limits:
- **Portfolio:** Up to 5 videos per account
- **Featured:** Only 1 video at a time
- **File size:** No limit (external hosting)

### Best Practices:
1. **High quality videos** - Professional appearance recommended
2. **Clear titles** - Describe what viewers will see
3. **Relevant content** - Match your business/service
4. **Appropriate length** - 30 seconds to 3 minutes ideal

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

### "Invalid URL" Error
- ✅ Use YouTube or Vimeo URLs only
- ✅ Full URL required (not shortened links)
- ✅ Examples:
  - `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ABC123`
  - `https://vimeo.com/123456789`

### "Upgrade Feature Coming Soon"
- This is expected for now
- Membership upgrades are in development
- For testing: Use accounts listed above with Silver/Gold tiers

### Featured Videos Not Showing on Homepage
- Videos must be **approved by admin** first
- Check if video shows "PENDING" or "FEATURED" badge
- May take a few moments to appear after approval
- Try refreshing the app

### Can't Find "Portfolio Videos" Section
- Make sure you're in **Edit Profile** mode (not view mode)
- Scroll down past other sections
- Collapsible section - tap to expand
- Available for **all user types** (business, entrepreneur, general public)

## 📞 QUESTIONS & SUPPORT

### How do I upgrade my membership?
Currently in development. The upgrade flow shows "Coming Soon" message. For testing purposes, contact support to manually upgrade accounts.

### How long does admin approval take?
Depends on admin availability. For testing, log in as Test_User to approve immediately.

### Can I feature multiple videos?
No, only 1 video can be featured at a time. You must unfeature the current video before featuring a different one.

### Will my featured video expire?
Not currently. Once approved, it stays featured until you unfeature it or admin removes it.
